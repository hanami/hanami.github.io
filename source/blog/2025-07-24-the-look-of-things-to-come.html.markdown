It’s week 6 of our sponsorship drive!

Let’s make sure you’re all caught up. So far, we have:

- [Launched the sponsorship drive](https://hanamirb.org/blog/2025/06/03/become-a-hanami-dry-and-rom-patron/)
- [Heard from our founding patrons](https://hanamirb.org/blog/2025/06/11/hear-from-our-founding-patrons/)
- [Shared a podcast and field report](https://hanamirb.org/blog/2025/06/20/field-report-from-riga-and-the-rooftop/)
- [Met Tim and Sean](https://hanamirb.org/blog/2025/06/26/meet-tim-and-sean/)
- [And addressed elephant in the room, Rails](https://hanamirb.org/blog/2025/07/11/hanami-and-the-elephant-in-the-room/)

There’s a lot of words across those posts. So today I want to share something different, something *visual*. Behold, cuteness!

![A preview of our new logos](/blog/2025/07/24/the-look-of-things-to-come/new-logos-preview.png "A preview of our new logos")

Here you have your very own preview of the new faces we’re creating for Hanami, Dry and Rom.

I’m *so* excited for this. These logos will unify our projects while allowing each to maintain its own identity. They’re also the visual representation of [the culture we’re building](/blog/2025/07/11/hanami-and-the-elephant-in-the-room/#it-all-comes-back-to-people), of kindness, curiosity and care — and also, delight! Ruby is fun, after all!

We’ll have more to share on this in due course, but if there’s one thing I already know, it’s that the future is looking bright (and cute!). We’d love to have you join in. 🌸

A huge thank you to [Aaron Moodie](https://aaronmoodie.com) for his work in bringing this to life!
